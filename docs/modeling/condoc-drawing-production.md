# ConDoc Drawing Production

**Last revised:** November 17, 2026

!!! note "Source and status"
    Imported from the November 17, 2026 conversation export. Its status labels are retained; some earlier decisions were recovered from summaries rather than complete original discussions. Proposed and unresolved material is not adopted office policy.

**Status:** Established workflow; complete configuration unavailable

The retained workflow includes the following approaches.

## Plans and sections

Use the established Donley-method workflow.

Hybrid rendering is required in the retained configuration.

## Exterior elevations

Use stacked viewports comprising:

1. Background shaded view
2. Linework and cut information
3. Ground line represented with heavier linework in a separate viewport

## Interior elevations

Use masked stacked viewports.

Masks are organized by room or relevant view.

## Site plans

Use stacked viewports to manage site information and graphic presentation.

## Unresolved

The complete ConDoc configurator, tag mappings, scene settings, and viewport rendering settings are not available for extraction.
