# Building Equipment Schedule

**Last revised:** November 24, 2026

## Provisional Template

The column structure is proposed and remains subject to refinement through project implementation.

**Columns:** `ID | TYPE | LOCATION | MANUFACTURER / MODEL | CAPACITY | NOTES`

**Drawing tag:** `EQ01`

**Schedule ID:** `EQ-01`

Standard width: **two horizontal grid modules**. Height varies with content and associated notes.

## Scope

Heat pumps, air handlers, mini-splits, ERVs/HRVs, dehumidifiers, water heaters, electrical panels, backup generators, transfer equipment, and propane tanks.

## Coordination

- Consolidate mechanical, plumbing, electrical, and fuel-system equipment into one architectural coordination schedule where appropriate.
- Coordinate equipment locations, required clearances, capacities, utilities, and relevant consultant documents.
- This architectural coordination schedule does not replace engineered equipment schedules, electrical panel schedules, load calculations, or technical system design.
- EQ replaces ME as the general Item Tag category for building equipment.
