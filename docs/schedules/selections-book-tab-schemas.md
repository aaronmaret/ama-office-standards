## Structural

Record design-impacting structural decisions, including:

- Foundation systems and exposed finishes
- Floor framing systems
- Roof framing systems
- Concealed and exposed beams
- Columns and posts
- Notable lateral-system requirements
- Cantilevers and large openings
- Stair structure

Exclude engineering calculations and detailed reinforcement design.

## Envelope

Record:

- Roofing systems
- Underlayment
- Ice protection
- Flashing
- Roof ventilation
- Gutters and downspouts
- Skylights
- Primary and accent cladding
- WRB
- Rainscreen strategy
- Insulation
- Exterior trim
- Soffits
- Exterior colors
- Masonry
- Deck systems

## Windows

Organize by family or condition, such as:

- Living-area windows
- Feature-view windows
- Bedroom windows
- Bathroom windows
- Clerestories

## Doors

Separate interior and exterior families.

Examples:

- Hinged interior doors
- Pocket doors
- Closet doors
- Primary entry doors
- Secondary exterior doors
- Patio doors

## HVAC

Record system strategy, equipment locations, distribution, visible grilles, thermostats, efficiency, noise, and indoor-air-quality considerations.

## Electrical Devices

Record:

- Switches
- Dimmers
- Receptacles
- GFCI devices
- USB devices
- Floor outlets
- Exterior outlets
- Data and Wi-Fi provisions
- Doorbells
- EV charging
- Garage controls

Avoid duplicating products governed by other tabs.

## Cabinetry

Organize by cabinet family and application.

Examples:

- Kitchen base cabinets
- Wall cabinets
- Tall cabinets
- Islands
- Open shelving
- Bathroom vanities
- Laundry cabinets
- Mudroom storage
- Media units
- Office built-ins

Custom Level values previously identified:

- Stock
- Semi-custom
- Fully custom

## Countertops

Maintain a separate selection category because countertop material, cost, edge treatment, and selection timing may differ from cabinetry.

## Trim

Record profile, material, finish, and size class.

Size classes:

- Small
- Medium
- Large
- Feature

## Finish Surfaces

Record finish families rather than producing a room-by-room CD finish schedule during DD.

## Hardware + Accessories

Include:

- Interior and exterior door hardware styles
- Pocket pulls
- Cabinet knobs and pulls
- Appliance pulls
- Bath accessories
- Shower-door hardware
- Closet rods
- Hooks
- Stair brackets

The CD Door Hardware Schedule remains authoritative for door hardware groups.

## Glass + Mirrors

Include shower enclosures, fixed panels, interior partitions, guards, bathroom mirrors, medicine cabinets, and specialty glazing.

## Exterior Fixtures

Include hose bibbs, exterior showers, specialty exterior devices, house numbers, mailboxes, doorbells, screening, railings, and related architectural accessories.

Avoid duplicating information governed by lighting, HVAC, or electrical documentation.

## Furniture

Include space-defining furniture rather than all furnishings.

Scale Class values:

- Compact
- Standard
- Oversized
- Feature

Source may identify:

- Owner
- Interior designer
- Custom fabrication
- Architect

## Window Treatments

Track:

- Treatment type
- Mounting strategy
- Light control
- Privacy
- Operation
- Power requirements

Emphasize early architectural coordination for recesses and electrical provisions.

## Allowances

Record explicit budget uncertainty.

Distinguish:

- Preliminary design estimates
- Actual construction-contract allowances

The amount, basis, unit, and responsible party must be clear.
