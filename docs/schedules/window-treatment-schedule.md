# Window Treatment Schedule

**Protocol:** SS-12  
**Last revised:** November 17, 2026

!!! note "Source and status"
    Imported from the November 17, 2026 conversation export. Its status labels are retained; some earlier decisions were recovered from summaries rather than complete original discussions. Proposed and unresolved material is not adopted office policy.

**Status:** Proposed / Conditional

## Scope

- Roller shades
- Blackout shades
- Draperies
- Blinds
- Motorized treatments
- Skylight shades

Include a Window Treatment Schedule in CDs only when the architect's scope includes specifying or coordinating the treatments.

Otherwise, architectural drawings should document relevant provisions:

- Recesses
- Blocking
- Power
- Control locations

Final product selections may remain with the owner or interior designer.
