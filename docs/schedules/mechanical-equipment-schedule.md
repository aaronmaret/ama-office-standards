# Mechanical Equipment Schedule

**Last revised:** November 18, 2026

## Scope

- Heat pumps
- Air handlers
- Mini-split equipment
- Energy recovery ventilators (ERVs)
- Heat recovery ventilators (HRVs)
- Dehumidifiers
- Dedicated ventilation equipment
- Thermostats and controls

## Recommended information

- Manufacturer
- Model
- Capacity
- Efficiency
- Electrical requirements
- Equipment location

No complete column order was established.

## Discipline coordination

Where a mechanical engineer provides an equipment schedule, that schedule should ordinarily govern.

Avoid duplicating authoritative equipment specifications in architectural documents.
