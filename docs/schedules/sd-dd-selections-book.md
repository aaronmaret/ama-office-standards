AMA maintains a Google Sheets selections book for residential SD/DD projects.

Its purpose is to track:

- Design intent
- Product families
- Options
- Cost priorities
- Client decisions
- Approval status

The selections book is not intended to become a complete CD product schedule.

## Established principles

- Organize information by product family or design condition.
- Avoid individual door/window instance IDs during early design.
- Avoid unnecessary quantities and exact dimensions.
- Avoid shop-drawing and procurement-level details during SD/DD.
- Maintain separate tabs for categories that benefit from independent selection decisions.
- Transition relevant decisions into authoritative CD schedules and drawings.

## Common schema approach

Most tabs use a variation of:

`Category | Item or Family | Application | Product / Material / Style Fields | Finish | Cost Tier | Status | Notes`

Each tab is refined for the relevant category.
