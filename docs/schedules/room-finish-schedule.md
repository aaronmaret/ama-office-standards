# Room Finish Schedule

**Last revised:** November 24, 2026

The Room Finish Schedule assigns materials and finishes by room and surface. Define the selections in the [Materials + Finishes Legend](materials-finishes-legend.md), then reference their IDs here.

FLOOR, WALLS, and CEILING are grouped headings. The row above illustrates the layout only.

## Entries

- Reference both material and finish IDs when a finish is separately specified, such as `WD-01` with `STF-01`.
- Use `-` in FINISH when the finish is integral to the material selection.
- Conventional substrate abbreviations such as `GYP` may be used without a separate legend entry.
- Use NOTES to reference numbered notes placed immediately below the schedule.
- Use `TBD` for unresolved selections and `-` for nonapplicable fields.

Standard width: **two horizontal grid modules**. Height varies with content and notes.
