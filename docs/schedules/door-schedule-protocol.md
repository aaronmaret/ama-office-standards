# Door Schedule Protocol

**Last revised:** November 17, 2026

!!! note "Source and status"
    Imported from the November 17, 2026 conversation export. Its status labels are retained; some earlier decisions were recovered from summaries rather than complete original discussions. Proposed and unresolved material is not adopted office policy.

**Status:** Confirmed in part; complete earlier protocol not directly accessible

The retained office protocol establishes the following conventions.

## Door identifiers

- `101`, `102`, etc.
- `201`, `202`, etc.

## Dimensions

Use inches.

## Door type

Use letters for door type identifiers.

## Hardware groups

Use numeric hardware group identifiers.

## Swing and handing

Show `LH` / `RH` handing on plans only.

## Door style and assembly terminology

The existing door schedule accommodates:

- Louvered door face styles
- Surface-sliding assemblies

## Specialty doors

Doors serving future equipment shafts or specialty spaces may require additional coordination.

No general rule was finalized in the accessible material for selecting hardware or assemblies before the future equipment system is known.

## General Door Notes

A set of General Door Notes was previously compiled, but the exact text is not accessible here.

Do not publish reconstructed General Door Notes as confirmed office standards without reviewing the original.
