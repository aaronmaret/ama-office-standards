## SD/DD

The Google Sheets selections book governs design-development tracking.

It records:

- Design intent
- Product families
- Options
- Cost priorities
- Selection status
- Client decisions

## CD / Construction

Architectural schedules and drawings establish:

- Specified products
- Locations
- Assemblies
- Installation requirements
- Relevant construction coordination

## Handoff principle

At CD development, relevant selections should be transferred into authoritative construction documentation.

However, not every selection must automatically leave the spreadsheet.

Owner-supplied appliances, furniture, window treatments, and deferred selections may remain active in the selections book or a separate procurement record.

The controlling document and contractual status must be clear.

Avoid conflicting specifications across the spreadsheet, architectural schedules, and consultant documents.
