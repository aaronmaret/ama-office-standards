# General Scheduling Philosophy

**Protocol:** SS-01  
**Last revised:** November 17, 2026

!!! note "Source and status"
    Imported from the November 17, 2026 conversation export. Its status labels are retained; some earlier decisions were recovered from summaries rather than complete original discussions. Proposed and unresolved material is not adopted office policy.

**Status:** Proposed

The recommended scheduling system is organized around how products are:

1. Selected
2. Specified
3. Purchased
4. Installed

The primary principle is:

Maintain one authoritative location for each selection, with other drawings referencing it.

Not every product requires its own schedule, and not every selection needs to appear in the architectural drawing set.

Schedules should remain appropriate to the scale and scope of the residential project.
