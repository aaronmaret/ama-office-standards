# Countertop + Backsplash Schedule

**Last revised:** November 24, 2026

## Provisional Template

The column structure is proposed and remains subject to refinement through project implementation.

**Columns:** `ID | TYPE | MATERIAL | FINISH | NOTES`

**Drawing tag:** `CT01 / BS01`

**Schedule ID:** `CT-01 / BS-01`

Standard width: **two horizontal grid modules**. Height varies with content and associated notes.

## Scope

Countertops and backsplashes coordinated as scheduled items.

## Coordination

- Coordinate cabinetry, plumbing fixtures, interior elevations, and the Materials + Finishes Legend.
- Reference material/finish selections rather than duplicating their specifications.
- Add fields or notes for thickness, edge profile, backsplash configuration, and termination conditions as needed.
- CT and BS identify items, not material classifications. Classification of engineered quartz, solid surface, and plastic laminate remains to be resolved.
- The SD/DD selections book retains its separate Countertops tab.
