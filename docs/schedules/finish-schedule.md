# Finish Schedule

**Last revised:** November 18, 2026

The recommendation was to incorporate countertops and backsplashes into a unified Finish Schedule for most residential projects.

## Scope

- Countertops
- Backsplashes
- Flooring
- Tile
- Wall finishes
- Ceiling finishes
- Specialty surface materials

## Proposed columns

`ID | Finish / Material | Application | Product / Reference | Remarks`

## Worked example

| ID    | Finish / Material | Application        | Product / Reference | Remarks        |
| ----- | ----------------- | ------------------ | ------------------- | -------------- |
| FN01 | Quartz            | Kitchen counters   | TBD                 | Eased edge     |
| FN02 | Ceramic tile      | Kitchen backsplash | TBD                 | See elevations |
| FN03 | White oak         | Living areas       | TBD                 | Natural finish |

## Drawing coordination

Architectural elevations and sections establish:

- Countertop heights
- Thicknesses
- Transitions
- Backsplash extents
- Termination conditions

Important distinction: The DD selections book retains a separate Countertops tab. The recommendation to combine countertops and backsplashes applies to CD scheduling, not necessarily to the DD spreadsheet structure.
