# Source and reliability

**Last revised:** November 17, 2026

## Imported source

The November 17, 2026 **AMA Office Standards — Conversation Export** supplied the content for 42 drawing, scheduling, modeling, and project-delivery protocols. The export combines directly available discussion with retained summaries of earlier discussions. It is not a complete verbatim record.

Status labels describe the source's reported level of agreement:

- **Confirmed:** Reported as an explicit decision or established office practice.
- **Proposed:** A recommendation awaiting adoption.
- **Unresolved:** An open question or incomplete protocol.
- **Partially confirmed / established:** Only the stated portions are supported; gaps remain visible on each page.

A confirmed workflow is distinct from a verified current software configuration. In particular, workbook schemas, status lists, and software workarounds require checking against the actual tools in use.

## Missing original material

The complete original door and window schedule fields, legends, general notes, and exceptions were unavailable. Also unavailable were the live selections workbook, detailed ConDoc configurations, full tag/scene and viewport procedures, earlier drawing-note wording, and field-report procedures.

These gaps have not been filled with invented requirements. See [decisions still needed](decisions-needed.md).

## Corrections to the starter site

The import replaces initial illustrative content with the recovered protocols:

- DD/CD sheet numbers use `A` plus two digits, such as `A00` and `A20`. The starter examples `A001`, `A201`, and `A601` are replaced.
- Drawing titles are proposed as **Drawing Type – Level or Area**, rather than the starter's level-first examples.
- Windows use instance identifiers such as `1.01`, with letters for window types. Doors use identifiers such as `101`, with letters for door types and numeric hardware groups.
- Schedules remain in A0, construction details in A6, diagrams in A7, and A8/A9 reserved. Historical assignments of schedules to A6 and details to A9 are superseded.

## Publication boundary

This edition includes general protocols and illustrative product examples. Internal template filenames and private business or client material are omitted. Draft content is visible for review and is not represented as adopted policy.
