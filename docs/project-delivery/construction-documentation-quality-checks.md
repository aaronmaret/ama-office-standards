# Construction Documentation Quality Checks

**Last revised:** November 18, 2026

The following checks arise directly from the scheduling and tagging recommendations.

## Identification

- Every scheduled item requiring a drawing reference has an appropriate identifier.
- Repeated products use consistent type identifiers.
- Unique assemblies use unique identifiers where necessary.
- Door and window IDs follow their established numbering protocols.

## Drawings

- Primary tags are placed on the appropriate drawings.
- Secondary tags are provided only when useful.
- Floor plans, elevations, and schedules agree.
- Equipment locations and required clearances are coordinated.
- RCP fixture locations align with architectural ceiling conditions.

## Specifications

- Product manufacturer and model information is coordinated.
- Finishes are consistent between schedules and drawings.
- Deferred selections are clearly identified.
- Owner-supplied items are distinguished where necessary.
- No conflicting authoritative specifications exist.

## Output

- Exported PDFs are reviewed.
- SketchUp/LayOut rendering discrepancies are checked.
- Sheet references and schedule locations reflect the current sheet organization.

This checklist is a proposed synthesis of the recommendations, not a separately approved QA/QC checklist.
