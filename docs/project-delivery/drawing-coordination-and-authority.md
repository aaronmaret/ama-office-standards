# Drawing Coordination and Authority

**Protocol:** PD-02  
**Last revised:** November 17, 2026

!!! note "Coordination guidance"

**Status:** Proposed

The recommended coordination hierarchy is:

- Architectural plans establish locations and spatial relationships.
- Elevations establish vertical relationships and architectural composition.
- Schedules establish specified products and relevant product attributes.
- Details establish construction assemblies and installation conditions.
- Manufacturer documentation provides product-specific requirements.
- Consultant drawings govern their respective engineered systems when included.
- Shop drawings establish fabrication information when required and approved.

Avoid contradictory requirements across these sources.

## Examples

Appliances: Coordinate the Appliance Schedule, floor plans, cabinet elevations, and manufacturer requirements.

Plumbing fixtures: Coordinate fixture schedules with architectural layouts and plumbing requirements.

Lighting: Coordinate RCPs, electrical drawings, fixture schedules, and ceiling conditions.

Shower glass: Coordinate architectural elevations, waterproofing, blocking, glass fabrication, and hardware.

Cabinetry: Coordinate plans, elevations, schedule entries, and shop drawings.
