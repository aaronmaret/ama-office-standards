# Drawing Set Organization

**Last revised:** November 18, 2026

Office protocol distinguishes between SD and DD/CD documentation.

## SD

Use a simplified architectural drawing set appropriate to the design phase.

Schematic Design phase drawings may use abbreviated sheet numbering such as:

- A1
- A2
- A3

Typical sheet size: **12 × 18 inches**.

## DD/CD

Use the expanded sheet number format using the series numbers as follows.

**Format:** `A(series digit).X` or `A(series digit).XX`

- **A** identifies architectural drawings.
- The **series digit** identifies the drawing category, using the [Sheet Series](#sheet-series) below.
- A **period** separates the series digit from the sheet sequence.
- The **sheet sequence** uses one or two numerals, depending on the project's size, complexity, and requirements.

Examples: `A2.1` or `A2.01` for a sheet in the A2 Plans series.

Typical sheet size: **24 × 36 inches**.

### Choosing the Sequence Length

Use two numerals when the sheet count in any series may exceed 10. This is usually the A2 Plans series: floor plans, MEP plans, framing plans, and reflected ceiling plans across two or more levels can push the series above 10 sheets. In that case, use numbers such as `A2.01`, `A2.02`, … `A2.11`.

## Sheet Series

Architectural drawing sheets are organized into the following series.

| Series | Category                               | Typical contents                                                                                                    |
| ------ | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A0     | General Information and Schedules      | Title sheet, drawing index, code information, general notes, door/window schedules, fixture and equipment schedules, project sheet specifications |
| A1     | Site                                   | Site plans, grading, drainage, site-related drawings                                                                |
| A2     | Plans                                  | Floor plans, roof plans, MEP plans, reflected ceiling plans                                                         |
| A3     | Exterior Elevations                    | Exterior elevations, exterior architectural views and 3D representations                                            |
| A4     | Sections                               | Building sections, wall sections, major building assemblies                                                         |
| A5     | Enlarged Plans and Interior Elevations | Enlarged kitchen and bathroom plans, interior elevations, millwork drawings, interior perspectives                  |
| A6     | Construction Details                   | Foundation, floor, wall, roof, opening, waterproofing, and other architectural details                              |
| A7     | Diagrams                               | Architectural diagrams, explanatory graphics, specialty coordination diagrams                                       |
| A8     | Reserved                               | Future expansion                                                                                                    |
| A9     | Reserved                               | Future expansion                                                                                                    |

## Numbering Examples

These examples illustrate the format; individual sheet assignments may vary by project.

| Series | One-Numeral Sequence | Two-Numeral Sequence | Example Content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A0 | A0.1 | A0.01 | General information |
| A0 | A0.2 | A0.02 | Door and window schedules |
| A0 | A0.3 | A0.03 | Fixture and equipment schedules |
| A1 | A1.1 | A1.01 | Site plan |
| A2 | A2.1 | A2.01 | Main level floor plan |
| A3 | A3.1 | A3.01 | Exterior elevations |
| A4 | A4.1 | A4.01 | Building sections |
| A5 | A5.1 | A5.01 | Enlarged plans and interior elevations |
| A6 | A6.1 | A6.01 | Construction details |
| A7 | A7.1 | A7.01 | Diagrams |

Smaller projects may combine multiple schedules on one sheet. Schedules belong in A0; A8 and A9 remain reserved.
