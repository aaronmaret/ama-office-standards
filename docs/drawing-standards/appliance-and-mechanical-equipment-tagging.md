# Appliance and Mechanical Equipment Tagging

**Protocol:** DS-08  
**Last revised:** November 17, 2026

!!! note "Source and status"
    Imported from the November 17, 2026 conversation export. Its status labels are retained; some earlier decisions were recovered from summaries rather than complete original discussions. Proposed and unresolved material is not adopted office policy.

**Status:** Proposed

## Appliances

Use floor plans as the primary tagging location.

Example:

`AP-01` — Refrigerator

The Appliance Schedule identifies the manufacturer, model, and relevant requirements.

Interior elevations supplement plan information where cabinet and appliance relationships require clarification.

## Mechanical equipment

- Tag equipment on mechanical plans when mechanical drawings are provided.
- Use architectural plans where equipment locations affect architectural coordination.
- Avoid duplicating mechanical equipment schedules between disciplines.
- Use sections or elevations when necessary to clarify clearances or spatial relationships.
