# Lighting and Fan Tagging

**Protocol:** DS-09  
**Last revised:** November 17, 2026

!!! note "Source and status"
    Imported from the November 17, 2026 conversation export. Its status labels are retained; some earlier decisions were recovered from summaries rather than complete original discussions. Proposed and unresolved material is not adopted office policy.

**Status:** Proposed

Use the Reflected Ceiling Plan (RCP) as the primary fixture-identification drawing.

## Suggested identifiers

- `L-01` — Lighting fixture type
- `L-02` — Lighting fixture type
- `F-01` — Fan type

Multiple fixtures of the same model may share a type identifier.

## Drawing responsibilities

RCP:

- Fixture locations
- Ceiling relationships
- Beams and soffits
- Architectural alignment
- Ceiling fans and exhaust fans

Electrical plan:

- Switching
- Circuits
- Controls
- Electrical coordination

For smaller residential projects, RCP and electrical information may be combined when legibility permits.

Wall-mounted fixtures may be more clearly located on interior or exterior elevations, especially where mounting height matters.
