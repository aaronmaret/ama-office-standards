# Cabinetry and Built-in Tagging

**Protocol:** DS-07  
**Last revised:** November 17, 2026

!!! note "Source and status"
    Imported from the November 17, 2026 conversation export. Its status labels are retained; some earlier decisions were recovered from summaries rather than complete original discussions. Proposed and unresolved material is not adopted office policy.

**Status:** Proposed

## Standard cabinetry

- Use interior elevations as the primary location for identification.
- Identify cabinet groups or families.
- Avoid assigning a schedule tag to every individual cabinet box unless the documentation requires it.
- Use floor plans to communicate cabinet arrangement and overall layout.

## Custom built-ins

Assign assembly identifiers such as:

- `BI-01`
- `BI-02`

Examples include:

- Storage benches
- Window seats
- Media cabinets
- Custom closet assemblies
- Integrated storage units

Tag these on plans and elevations where needed.

## Documentation hierarchy

- Floor plans establish arrangement.
- Interior elevations establish composition, dimensions, finishes, and relationships to appliances.
- Shop drawings establish fabrication information when required.
