# Receptacle Identification

**Last revised:** November 18, 2026

Use standardized electrical symbols rather than unique receptacle identifiers.

Recommended symbol categories:

- Standard duplex receptacle
- GFCI-protected receptacle
- Weather-resistant exterior receptacle
- Floor receptacle
- Dedicated appliance receptacle
- 240V receptacle
- USB charging receptacle

Use keyed device tags only for specialty products or unusual installation requirements.

Interior elevations should supplement the power plan where precise relationships matter, including:

- Kitchen backsplashes
- Bathroom vanities
- Built-in desks
- Cabinet-integrated devices

Device families and finishes may be established in the Electrical Devices selections documentation and general electrical notes.
